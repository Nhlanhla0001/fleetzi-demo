The current public workspace uses fictional fleet data so the product can be explored without a live customer account or connected equipment.

Demo fleet

The product demonstration stores its fictional fleet state in this browser so the experience can continue as you move between pages. It does not create a customer account or connect to real equipment.

Do not enter personal information, confidential agreements, real device identifiers or other sensitive information into the public demonstration.

Installation planner

The installation planner prepares a text file on your device. It does not send the brief or confirm an appointment. You decide whether and how to share the downloaded file.

Hosting and page resources

Images and fonts are served with the site. The current site code includes no advertising trackers or analytics scripts. The hosting provider may process standard request information to serve and secure the website.

Illustrative content

Example machines, readings, customers, rates, locations and service targets are fictional demonstration data. Site photography is illustrative. No displayed example establishes a real installation, customer relationship or guaranteed equipment capability.

Before live service

A connected customer service requires confirmed service terms, appropriate privacy information, access controls, secure device provisioning and verified installation. Fleetzi does not replace machine safety procedures, inspections or emergency response processes.
