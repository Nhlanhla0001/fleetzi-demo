Fleet management.
Built around the machine.

Fleetzi gives plant and heavy-equipment owners one fleet workspace, then one operational record for each machine.

Fleet visibility.
All equipment together.

Manage the equipment register and see current or last-known fleet position while keeping each machine as its own persistent asset record.

  • Fleet register and status
  • Live fleet map and movement history
  • Sites and geofences
  • Current, stale and offline state

Utilisation, fuel
and diagnostics.

Combine location with verified machine activity. Available fields depend on the machine and installation.

  • Working, idle and engine-running history
  • Utilisation over time
  • Hour-meter and distance history
  • Fuel and diagnostics where supported

Maintenance,
inspections and alerts.

Operational work belongs to the exact machine record and rolls up to fleet-level queues.

  • Service due and overdue state
  • Maintenance and work-order history
  • Pre-start inspection status
  • Alerts, exceptions and operator actions

Fleet records
with commercial context.

Plant hire adds customer, site and agreement context to normal fleet management.

  • Customer and site allocation
  • Agreed rates and minimum hire terms
  • Measured activity kept separate from proposed billable hours

The right installation
comes first.

Not every machine exposes the same data.

Available readings depend on the equipment, supported connections, sensors and installation. Compatibility is checked before installation and only verified machine information is shown in the fleet record.
